About

A local painter for Albany houses.

EDIT: ABOUT — Al Ruelas Painting is a residential painting company based in Albany, Oregon. We take on interior and exterior house painting for homeowners who want careful prep, honest scheduling, and a finish that still looks good next year.

We show up as quoted

Clear scope, written estimate, and a start date you can plan around. If something changes on site, we talk before we paint.

Prep is not optional

Oregon weather finds weak spots. We wash, scrape, sand, and prime so the topcoat has something to hold onto.

Respect for the house

Drop cloths, clean edges, and a walkthrough at the end. We leave the job looking like painters were here — not a construction site.

Right paint for the job

Quality interior and exterior products suited to siding, trim, kitchens, and high-traffic rooms. Brand preference? Say so on the estimate form.

Questions

Before you book

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We look at the house in person so the number matches the actual prep, not a guess from a photo.

Can we stay in the house?

For most interior jobs, yes. We work room by room and keep walkways open. We’ll tell you if a space needs to be emptied.

What about rain?

Exterior work waits for a dry window. We don’t trap moisture under new paint just to keep a calendar date.
